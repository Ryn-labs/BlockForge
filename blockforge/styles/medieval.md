# Medieval style

## Palette
- Walls: cobblestone, stone_bricks, mossy_stone_bricks (castle); white_terracotta / birch_planks / stripped oak plaster (timber houses)
- Frame: oak_log, spruce_log (half-timbering with light plaster infill); dark_oak_log only for a stronghold or a dark build
- Roof: oak_stairs, spruce_stairs, or teal / red with an oak trim for a manor; stone_brick_stairs (towers), cobblestone_slab caps; dark_oak or deepslate roofs only when asked for a dark look
- Floor: oak_planks, spruce_planks, cobblestone, stone_bricks, packed dirt_path outside
- Trim: oak_fence, stripped_oak_log beams, stone_brick_wall battlements, cobblestone_wall
- Accents: banners, barrels, hay_block, iron_bars windows, oak_trapdoor shutters, chains
- Lighting: lantern hung from beams, wall torches on stone, campfire in hearths
- Plants: oak_leaves trees, hay and flower beds on grass, vines on stone, ferns

## Proportions
- Storey height 4 (3 free blocks); upper storeys of timber houses jut out 1 block (jetty).
- Roof pitch steep: 1 up per 1 across (45 degrees) for houses, conical or pyramid caps for towers.
- Overhang 1 block; chimney 1 block above the ridge.
- Window rhythm: single 1x2 window every 3-4 blocks, small and shuttered; arrow slits in castle walls.
- Castle walls 3 thick, 7-10 high, crenellated (alternate full and empty on top), towers 5x5 to 7x7.

## Typical details
- Half-timber: log corners and diagonal or crossed beams over white plaster walls.
- Stone ground floor, timber upper floor; stone chimney with a campfire hearth.
- Castle: gatehouse with portcullis (iron_bars), open courtyard inside, wall walk behind battlements.
- Market square of packed paths, wells, hay carts, banners.

## Common mistakes
1. Flat roofs on houses; use steep stair roofs closed with no gaps.
2. Perfect smooth walls; mix cobblestone, mossy stone and stone bricks.
3. Closing a castle courtyard with buildings; keep the courtyard open.
4. Modern blocks (concrete, quartz, glass blocks, smooth stone) in the walls.
5. Doors blocked by steps or fences, rooms without light, towers without a way up.
6. A timber house that reads near-black (dark oak frame, spruce fill, deepslate roof): keep the infill light and the roof coloured unless the user asked for a dark build.
