# Modern style

## Palette
- Walls: white_concrete, light_gray_concrete, smooth_quartz, polished_andesite plinths
- Frame: black_concrete, gray_concrete, blackstone or stripped_dark_oak_log mullions
- Glass: glass, light_gray_stained_glass, tinted_glass; large panes in 3x3 or taller sheets
- Roof: flat roof of smooth_stone_slab / stone_slab with a 1-block parapet; or a single-pitch slab roof
- Floor: smooth_stone, light_gray_concrete, polished_andesite, oak_planks / birch_planks warm interior floor
- Trim: quartz_slab edges, black_concrete lines, iron_door, spruce_trapdoor
- Accents: warm wood panels (oak, birch), a green wall of moss_block, a dark accent block
- Lighting: sea_lantern or glowstone behind trapdoors/slabs, lantern strips, end_rod, light blocks hidden in ceilings
- Plants: grass lawn, flowering azalea in pots, small birch trees, hedges of leaves

## Proportions
- Storey height 5 (4 free blocks) for airy rooms; cantilevered upper volume overhangs 2-4 blocks.
- Flat or single-pitch roof, overhang 1-2, thin slab edge.
- Window rhythm: floor-to-ceiling glass bands, 3+ wide, interrupted by solid planes.
- Mix 2-3 boxes at different heights and offsets rather than one block.

## Typical details
- Cantilever with a visible support column or a hidden steel line (black_concrete).
- Garage, terrace with glass railings (glass_pane), pool with sea lantern lights.
- Clean paved driveways in smooth_stone or polished_andesite, hedges as borders.

## Common mistakes
1. Plain box with no volume change; offset and stack the volumes.
2. Cobblestone, logs and wood stairs mixed in; keep the palette to 3 colours.
3. Glass walls with no floor edge or frame; lines make it read as modern.
4. Dark, unlit interiors; modern rooms still need light level 8+ everywhere.
5. Roof stairs on a flat design; use slabs and parapets.
