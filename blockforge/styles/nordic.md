# Nordic style

## Palette
- Walls: spruce_log, stripped_spruce_log, spruce_planks, dark_oak_planks for plank-and-log longhouses; stone and cobblestone base
- Frame: dark_oak_log, spruce_log; carved dragon-head gable ends with spruce_fence and trapdoors
- Roof: spruce_stairs and dark_oak_stairs, with grass_block / moss_block / podzol turf on top for turf roofs; thatch = dried_kelp_block
- Floor: spruce_planks, packed dirt, podzol, coarse_dirt, cobblestone
- Trim: spruce_fence, spruce_trapdoor, stripped_spruce_log, chains
- Accents: shields (banners), barrels, furs (white / gray wool carpet), red_wool banners, iron_bars
- Lighting: lantern, campfire central hearth, wall torches, soul_lantern for cold outdoor posts
- Plants: spruce and birch, ferns, sweet_berry_bush, moss, short_grass, podzol floors

## Proportions
- Longhouse 5-7 wide and 12-20 long, 4 high walls; roof steep (1 up per 1 across) almost to the ground.
- Overhang 1-2; ridge crossed by carved beam ends.
- Window rhythm: few windows, small, high, shuttered; door in the gable end or long side with a porch.
- Docks and boathouses: stilts of logs standing in or on the ground.

## Typical details
- Central fire pit (campfire with smoke hole in the roof above it).
- Palisade of vertical spruce logs and fences, watchtower with a banner.
- Longboat shed, drying racks, stacked firewood, rune stones (cobblestone with chiseled stone).

## Common mistakes
1. A hearth with no smoke hole; leave a 1-block gap or a chimney in the ridge above the campfire.
2. A longhouse that reads black: keep spruce for the frame and roof, but break the walls with lighter infill (stripped birch, birch planks or white plaster panels) and warm lit windows unless the user asked for a dark build.
3. Turf roofs with grass_block placed directly on stairs; use moss_block or podzol and slabs.
4. Stilts or docks floating over water without posts in the ground.
5. Lantern posts and hanging lanterns with no beam or ground under them.
