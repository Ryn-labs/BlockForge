# Desert style

## Palette
- Walls: sandstone, smooth_sandstone, cut_sandstone, chiseled_sandstone, white_terracotta (plaster), terracotta, red_sandstone
- Frame: stripped_acacia_log, acacia_planks, dark_oak_log for beams
- Roof: flat roofs of sandstone_slab / smooth_sandstone_slab with parapet; domes of white_concrete or sandstone
- Floor: smooth_sandstone, cut_sandstone, terracotta, orange_terracotta, sand outside
- Trim: sandstone_wall, cut_sandstone_slab, acacia_fence, acacia_trapdoor shutters
- Accents: blue_terracotta / cyan_terracotta tiles, red_carpet and banners, orange_concrete awnings, gold_block on domes
- Lighting: lantern hung from beams, campfire, torch on walls, glowstone under floors
- Plants: cactus on sand, dead_bush, palms = jungle_log trunks with jungle_leaves, bamboo, date palms by wells

## Proportions
- Storey height 4; thick walls (2) with small deep windows.
- Flat roofs with a 1 block parapet and 1 block overhang; domes radius 3-5 on towers.
- Window rhythm: small arched openings every 3-4 blocks, bigger arches on the street level.
- Towers (minarets) 3x3 and 2-3 times house height, with a balcony.

## Typical details
- Terraced rooftops, awnings of orange and white carpets laid over fences.
- Courtyard with a well, palms and cloth shade; arched doorways in sandstone stairs and slabs.
- Market stalls with banners and barrels; streets of smooth sandstone or packed dirt_path.

## Common mistakes
1. Using sand blocks as walls; sand falls. Use sandstone.
2. Pitched stair roofs; flat roofs and domes are typical.
3. Cactus placed on sandstone or in the air; it must sit on sand.
4. No shade or colour; add terracotta tiles and cloth.
5. Doors and arches with no headroom (need 2 clear above the floor).
