# Fantasy style

## Palette
- Walls: white_concrete / quartz_block / calcite (elven); deepslate_bricks / blackstone (dark); amethyst accents; stripped_birch_log
- Frame: stripped_birch_log, stripped_cherry_log, dark_oak_log for twisted tree-houses
- Roof: purple / blue / teal dyed terracotta or copper-family stairs (oxidized_cut_copper_stairs), prismarine_brick_stairs, spiral cone roofs (deepslate / blackstone only for a dark or spooky build)
- Floor: polished_diorite, moss_block, white_terracotta, amethyst_block patches
- Trim: end_rod, chain, iron_bars, stripped logs, quartz_pillar
- Accents: amethyst_cluster, glow_berries, sea_lantern, enchanting_table, banners
- Lighting: lantern, sea_lantern, glowstone, shroomlight, end_rod, candles in groups
- Plants: giant mushrooms (red / brown), cherry_leaves, azalea, glow_lichen, vines, moss_carpet

## Proportions
- Tall and thin: towers 3-5 times taller than wide, storey height 5-6.
- Steep, curved conical roofs (slope 2 up per 1 across), big overhang 2-3 at the cap.
- Window rhythm: tall arched windows (2x4) spaced irregularly; round windows in towers.
- Asymmetry is good, but each part must stay connected and supported.

## Typical details
- Spiral stairs around a tower with 2 free blocks above every step and a landing each turn.
- Bridges between towers on arches; floating islands only with a visible chain or root as support.
- Glowing accents in a single colour; mushroom houses with round caps.

## Common mistakes
1. Random coloured blocks everywhere; choose 2 main hues and one glow accent.
2. Floating towers with no support; give every part a base, roots, or chains.
3. Spiral stairs with 90 degree turns and no headroom.
4. Square windows and flat roofs; use arches and cones.
5. Too bright everywhere; keep a few dark areas and put light on features.
