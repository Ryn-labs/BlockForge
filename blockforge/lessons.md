# Build lessons (top 40)

## Support and placement
- Support every part: nothing may float; each group of blocks must touch the ground or a supported block.
- Hang lanterns from a beam, chain or block above them, or stand them on a block below (hanging=true only with something above).
- Put torches on a floor block or on the side of a solid wall (wall_torch with facing away from the wall).
- Plant grass, flowers, saplings and ferns on dirt, grass_block or podzol; cactus on sand.
- Never use sand or gravel as a wall or roof; they fall. Use sandstone or stone.
- Never fill a big box and then carve it out in pieces if it leaves stubs; build hollow shells or clear the whole interior.

## Paths and stairs
- Never put a path block inside terrain; every path cell needs 2 air blocks above it.
- Make every 1-block rise a bottom stair facing uphill (its high side points up the hill).
- Turn on a flat landing, or turn gradually; never turn 90 degrees on a single stair.
- Curve stair corners at 15-25 degrees per step so the road bends smoothly.
- Give long stairs a flat landing every 6-8 steps.
- Leave 2 free blocks of headroom over every stair and slab step; no beam, fence or roof there.
- Cut roads into earth banks (sloped dirt and grass) instead of tall walls beside the road.

## Doors and interiors
- Keep the blocks in front of and behind every door clear; nothing solid there.
- Build doors as a lower and upper half with the same facing and hinge.
- Put each house door at the ground's level (or one stair step up); make every room reachable by walking, 2 free blocks overhead.
- Place windows so they do not face a wall or a neighbouring building.
- Stairs inside need a hole in the floor above and 2 free blocks over every step.
- Furnish rooms with a few useful items (bed, table, chest) instead of leaving them empty.

## Roofs
- Close the roof with no gaps: gable ends filled, ridge capped, overhang on every side.
- Match the roof pitch to the style (steep medieval, low wide Japanese, shallow alpine).
- Give roofs an overhang of 1-3 blocks and put the eave stairs on the wall line, not inside it.
- Roof stairs face downhill on the slope: the high side toward the ridge.

## Lighting
- Light every room (a lantern every 6 blocks inside) and every path (light level 8 or more) so mobs cannot spawn.
- Put outdoor light on posts, eaves and gateways, one every 6-8 blocks along paths.
- Hide light sources in the style: lanterns for old styles, sea lanterns or light blocks for modern.

## Terrain and nature
- Build on the ground: start walls at the real terrain height and fill foundations down to it.
- Use real terrain from the world or a heightmap; do not put a flat slab over hills.
- Blend edges: add grass, dirt, gravel and plants where a build meets the terrain.
- Mix tree sizes and species; keep leaves persistent so they do not decay.
- Keep castle and compound courtyards open; do not fill the middle with buildings.

## Size and proportion
- Build the size the user asked for: check the bounds before saving.
- Keep storeys 4 blocks tall (3 free) unless the style needs more.
- Break plain walls with pillars, trim, windows or a base course; use 3-5 materials.

## Editing
- Do not overwrite finished parts; read the overwrite report and check what changed.
- Edit parts of a build with small steps and undo a step that goes wrong.
- Check, view and walk_test after every part before moving on.
- Put sign text in short lines (under 15 characters each) and use real wording.
- Do not use modern blocks (concrete, quartz, glass blocks) in old styles.
- Keep the palette to the style sheet; ask for the style sheet before building.
