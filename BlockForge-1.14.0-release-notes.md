## BlockForge 1.14.0: it improves its own builds, and houses look like real houses

**It fixes its own work.** After every step the build is checked and scored. If something is off, BlockForge goes back and improves it by itself, up to three rounds, and shows you what it is doing ("Improving: roof overhang, round 1 of 3"). When it hands the build back it tells you what got better, for example "quality 35 -> 71".

**Bright, timber-framed houses by default.** Light wood walls in an oak log frame, teal or coloured roofs with trim, white-framed windows with flower boxes underneath. Dark looks only when you ask for them (dark, gothic, spooky...).

**Judged by how it looks.** The quality check now looks at the finished build from all four sides, the way you would: how light the walls are, how the frame stands out, how colourful the roof is. A house that reads dark or plain is improved before it is handed over.

**For AI chats.** When another AI builds through BlockForge, every result says plainly whether the build is done or what still needs fixing, so builds are not handed over half-finished.

Install by running `BlockForge-1.14.0.msi` (or press **Update** in the app). No restart needed. Your settings, keys and builds are kept.
