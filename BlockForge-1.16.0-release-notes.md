## BlockForge 1.16.0: faster big builds and roads that follow the land

**Faster.** Big builds on large terrain are much quicker: each building step, check and review only looks at what changed, and results are reused until the build changes.

**Roads that follow the land.** Streets now follow the ground with gentle rises and landings at the corners, keep head room clear and never run into the hillside. Paths buried under paving are caught and fixed.

**Softer window boxes.** Flower boxes use mixed, softer flowers on some of the windows, so houses no longer show rows of bright red squares.

Install by running `BlockForge-1.16.0.msi` (or press **Update** in the app). No restart needed. Your settings, keys and builds are kept.
