## BlockForge 1.17.0: natural rugged mountains

**Mountains that look natural.** New mountains have several summits and saddles, a blunt main peak set off-centre, and irregular ledges and shoulders that flatten out left and right at different heights. Snow lies on the ledges and gentler ground above an uneven snowline, while steep faces, streaks and patches show dark stone. No flat cut edges and no sharp perfect cones.

**More control.** The terrain tool takes optional ruggedness, ledges and snow mix settings; the default is the new look.

**Better terrain score.** The terrain check now spots land that looks too perfect: a symmetric cone, slopes that are all the same, or one clean white cap.

**Long builds finish.** A big step-by-step build that reaches the step limit now keeps its build and finishes improving it instead of stopping with an error, and a usage-limit message only appears when there really is one.

Install by running `BlockForge-1.17.0.msi` (or press **Update** in the app). No restart needed. Your settings, keys and builds are kept.
