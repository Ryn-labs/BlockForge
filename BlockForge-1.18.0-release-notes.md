## BlockForge 1.18.0: houses that work for players

**House check.** Every building in a build is now checked the way a player would use it: the door can be reached from outside without jumping, the rooms inside can be walked through with enough head room, no spot inside is dark enough for mobs to spawn, the roof is closed, and the house sits on the land (no walls hanging over air, no earth piled up the walls). Each problem comes with its place and how to fix it, and the AI fixes them before it saves.

**Better built-in houses.** Framed houses now light their rooms, spiral towers get torches and a flat landing at the door, and the example cottage and village have doors into every wing.

**3D viewer.** A single click (or tap on a phone) now shows a block's name; a double-click highlights every block of that kind. A stray click while looking around no longer turns the rest of the build into faint ghosts.

**Safe on huge builds.** Very large or spread-out builds skip the house check with a note instead of slowing the checks down.

Install by running `BlockForge-1.18.0.msi` (or press **Update** in the app). No restart needed. Your settings, keys and builds are kept.
