## BlockForge 1.15.0: real manors and natural mountains

**Houses with real shape.** Big houses now get a tall central block with a front gable, wings stepping down on both sides, balconies, a grand stair and varied roof heights. Small houses get a porch, a chimney and a loft. Long, low boxes don't pass the quality check any more.

**Natural mountains.** Make mountains, ranges, valleys and hills that look like the real thing: gentle slopes, ridges and gullies, rock faces, scree at the foot, a snow cap and forests that thin out with height. No more blocky steps or cut walls.

**Clear windows.** Windows are clear glass in a light frame, with flowers only in the box underneath.

**Ready-made parts in every build.** The proven parts (manors, framed houses, stairs, roofs, balconies and more) can now be used directly while building, so good results come the first time.

**Clearer chat.** The plan card always shows for builds with several parts and stays up to date after the builder improves its own work.

Install by running `BlockForge-1.15.0.msi` (or press **Update** in the app). No restart needed. Your settings, keys and builds are kept.
